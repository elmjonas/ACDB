Supporting data for the work:
"Modeling the Aerosol Aqueous Phase: Solvation of Pyruvic Acid Analogs and Na+, Cl- Ions".

The systems for which the data was obtained follows the notation:
w = water
na = sodium (+)
cl = chloride (-)
dhppa = 2,2-dihydroxypropanoic acid
lca = lactic acid
ppa = propionic acid
pua = pyruvic acid
pue = pyruvate 

Structures were obtained at DFT level of theory employing the wB97X-D/6-31++G(d,p) methodology with the IEFPCM solvation method.
Single-point electronic energy corrections are calculated at DLPNO-CCSD(T_0)/aug-cc-pVTZ level of theory.

For more info on how to understand the file format, see https://github.com/elmjonas/ACDB/blob/master/README.md
