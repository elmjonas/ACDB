Supporting data for the work:
"Modeling the Clustering of Fumaric/Maleic Acid with Water and Na+, Cl- Ions".

The systems for which the data was obtained follows the notation:
w = water
na = sodium (+)
cl = chloride (-)
fua = fumaric acid
maa = maleic acid
fuc = fumarate monoanion (-)
mac = maleate monoanion (-)
fucc = fumarate dianion (2-)
macc = maleate dianion (2-)

Structures were obtained at DFT level of theory employing the wB97X-D/6-31++G(d,p) methodology in the gas and aqueous phase and with the IEFPCM solvation method for the aqueous phase.
Single-point electronic energy corrections are calculated at DLPNO-CCSD(T_0)/aug-cc-pVTZ level of theory.

For more info on how to understand the file format, see https://github.com/elmjonas/ACDB/blob/master/README.md
